5. Why did the line for Milkshake change? Hint: Click on the icon next to “Interest
over time” for more information about how trend results are displayed.
The Milkshake line changed because Google Trends uses relative popularity, not actual search counts. When Hamburger was added, the chart rescaled both terms on the same scale, so Milkshake's values were adjusted based on the highest search interest between the two searches. This made the Milkshake line appear different.

7. What does all of this suggest about the reliability of the conclusions reached in the
article?
The article's conclusions should be viewed with caution because Google Trends measures search interest, not actual food choices. A food may receive more searches for many reasons unrelated to breakups. The results can provide insights into search behavior, but they do not prove what people actually eat after a breakup.
